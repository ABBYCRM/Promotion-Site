What people in Orlando usually need first

Local pages exist so the site can hold genuine corridor, agency, and data context over time. The immediate practical layer is the same across Florida: preserve evidence, organize records, and reduce uncertainty before insurance conversations accelerate.

Practical starting list

  • Photograph the scene, vehicles, and roadway conditions when safe.
  • Capture witness names and contact information while they are present.
  • Note responding agencies and any report or case numbers given.
  • Keep medical, towing, and repair documents in one place.
  • Write a short dated account of what you remember.
