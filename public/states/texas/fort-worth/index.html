THE FIRST-HOURS CHECKLIST

Preserve evidence now. Decide later.

Uncertainty creates delay. A short checklist reduces the mental load while information is still accessible.

  1. 01

    Photograph vehicles, plates, road conditions and visible injuries

  2. 02

    Save witness names and contact information

  3. 03

    Keep medical, towing, repair and insurance records together

  4. 04

    Write down what you remember before details fade

WHY THIS MATTERS

You do not need to decide today.

You can preserve information now and decide what to do with it later. The difficult direction is the reverse.
