About Florida Crash Guide

Learn about Florida Crash Guide — an independent educational resource helping accident victims understand their options. Supporting CaseClosedFL's free intake.

We help people understand their options after a Florida accident.

Florida Crash Guide is an independent educational resource. We provide practical information about what to do after a motor vehicle accident in Florida — organized around what actually matters, not what fills a brochure.

Our goal is to reduce the uncertainty people feel immediately after a crash. We help connect eligible individuals with CaseClosedFL's free accident eligibility intake — a service that matches potential claimants with attorneys based on the specifics of their situation.

Who is CaseClosedFL?

CaseClosedFL provides a free accident eligibility intake. It is not a law firm. Over 5,000 people have used the service. The intake is designed to help determine whether a person's accident may fit the criteria for connecting with an attorney. No result or attorney match is guaranteed.

Our approach

Every piece of information on this site is organized around what people in actual accident situations commonly need. We cite primary sources (FLHSMV, NHTSA, FDOT) wherever possible rather than manufacturing certainty. Our CTAs are transparent: the free intake is free, and we say so plainly.
