THE FIRST-HOURS CHECKLIST

Future-you may need what present-you can still preserve.

Uncertainty creates delay. A short checklist reduces the mental load and gives you something concrete to do while information is still accessible.

  1. 01

    Photograph vehicles, plates, road conditions and visible injuries

  2. 02

    Save witness names and contact information

  3. 03

    Keep medical, towing, repair and insurance records together

  4. 04

    Write down what you remember before details fade

  5. 05

    Avoid guessing about injuries or fault when you do not know

WHY THIS MATTERS

You do not need to decide everything today.

You can preserve information now and decide what to do with it later. The difficult direction is the reverse: trying to recreate missing evidence weeks afterward.
